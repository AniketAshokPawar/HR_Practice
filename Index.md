HR + Behavioral Interview Questions for QA ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
================================================

1\. Introduction & Background ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
-----------------------------------

These are almost guaranteed:

1.  **Tell me about yourself.**
2.  **Walk me through your resume.**
3.  **Tell me about your current role and responsibilities.**
4.  **Explain your current project.**
5.  **What is your total experience?**
6.  **How much experience do you have in Manual and Automation Testing?**
7.  **What technologies/tools are you currently working with?**
8.  **What are your day-to-day responsibilities?**

* * * * *

2\. Current Job & Switching ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
=================================

Very commonly asked in the first HR call:

1.  **Why are you looking for a job change?**
2.  **Why do you want to leave your current company?**
3.  **Why are you interested in this opportunity?**
4.  **Why do you want to join our company?**
5.  **What are you looking for in your next role?**
6.  **Why should we hire you?**
7.  **What are your career goals?**
8.  **Where do you see yourself in the next 3--5 years?**

* * * * *

3\. Notice Period & Availability ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
======================================

Almost always asked:

1.  **What is your current notice period?**
2.  **When can you join?**
3.  **Is your notice period negotiable?**
4.  **Have you resigned already?**
5.  **Can you join immediately / earlier than your notice period?**

⚠️ Prepare answers carefully based on your actual situation.

* * * * *

4\. Salary & Offer Questions ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
==================================

Very common in HR rounds:

1.  **What is your current CTC?**
2.  **What is your current in-hand salary?**
3.  **What are your salary expectations?**
4.  **Do you have any other offers?**
5.  **What is the expected CTC from your side?**
6.  **Are you flexible regarding salary?**
7.  **What is the minimum package you are expecting?**

* * * * *

5\. Behavioral Questions ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
==============================

These indirectly test your **communication, attitude, and problem-solving**.

1.  **Tell me about a challenging situation you faced at work.**
2.  **Tell me about a difficult bug you found.**
3.  **Tell me about a situation where you disagreed with a developer.**
4.  **How do you handle conflicts with team members?**
5.  **Tell me about a mistake you made and what you learned from it.**
6.  **Tell me about a failure or difficult situation in your career.**
7.  **How do you handle pressure and tight deadlines?**
8.  **How do you handle multiple tasks at the same time?**
9.  **What do you do when requirements are unclear?**
10. **What if a developer rejects your bug?**
11. **What if you disagree with a developer about bug severity?**
12. **How do you handle criticism or feedback?**

* * * * *

6\. Teamwork & Communication ⭐⭐⭐⭐
=================================

1.  **How do you work with developers?**
2.  **How do you communicate bugs to developers?**
3.  **How do you communicate with your manager or Product Owner?**
4.  **How do you handle misunderstandings within the team?**
5.  **Do you prefer working independently or in a team?**
6.  **How do you handle different opinions in a team?**
7.  **Have you ever helped a teammate?**
8.  **How do you handle knowledge sharing within your team?**

* * * * *

7\. Situation-Based Questions ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
===================================

These are important for a QA with experience:

1.  **You have very little time for testing. What will you do?**
2.  **A critical bug is found before release. What will you do?**
3.  **Production has an issue. How will you handle it?**
4.  **Requirements change during testing. What will you do?**
5.  **A developer says the bug is not reproducible. What will you do?**
6.  **You find many bugs just before release. What will you do?**
7.  **You are assigned an unfamiliar feature. How will you start testing?**
8.  **Your manager asks you to test something urgently while you already have another task. What will you do?**
9.  **You missed an important bug. What would you do?**

* * * * *

8\. Strengths & Weaknesses ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
================================

1.  **What are your strengths?**
2.  **What is your biggest weakness?**
3.  **What makes you different from other candidates?**
4.  **What motivates you?**
5.  **What kind of work environment do you prefer?**

⚠️ Don't give fake answers like:

> "My weakness is that I work too hard." 😂

We can prepare a genuine but safe answer.

* * * * *

9\. Company & Role Questions ⭐⭐⭐⭐
=================================

1.  **What do you know about our company?**
2.  **Why do you want to join our company?**
3.  **Why are you interested in this role?**
4.  **What attracted you to this job description?**
5.  **How does your experience match this position?**

* * * * *

10\. HR Verification / Closing Questions ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
==============================================

1.  **Are you comfortable with hybrid/office work?**
2.  **Are you willing to relocate?**
3.  **What is your current location?**
4.  **Are you comfortable with the job location?**
5.  **Are you comfortable with the working hours?**
6.  **Are you comfortable working in shifts?**
7.  **Do you have any competing offers?**
8.  **Do you have any questions for us?**

* * * * *
