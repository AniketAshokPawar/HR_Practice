### Q.2 Current role & responsibilities.

In my current role, I work as a Manual and Automation Test Engineer, and I am involved in both functional testing and automation testing.

From the Agile side, I participate in activities such as daily stand-ups, sprint planning, backlog refinement or grooming, sprint reviews, and retrospectives. During grooming, I understand the requirements and acceptance criteria and provide my inputs from the testing perspective.

For manual testing, based on the acceptance criteria, I create test cases and prepare the required test data. I then execute the test cases and validate the functionality against the requirements. If I find any issues, I report defects with proper details such as the title, description, environment, severity, priority, screenshots, and videos. I also perform integration testing and regression testing, especially during the later sprints of the PI.

On the automation side, I primarily work with Playwright and TypeScript. I create new end-to-end automation scripts for new functionalities. Depending on the requirement, I may also modify the Page Object code or test data if the new workflow requires it. I also analyze and fix existing automation scripts when they fail.

Overall, my role involves understanding requirements, testing the functionality manually, automating important end-to-end workflows, reporting and retesting defects, and supporting regression testing to ensure the quality of the product.

### Q.3 Explain your current project.

Currently, I am working on **Designcenter X**, Siemens' cloud-based CAD solution, which is used for designing 3D parts and assemblies, including sheet-metal components, and creating 2D engineering drawings from them.

In my project, I mainly test functionalities related to **holes and datum features in the 3D environment**, and annotations such as **Datum Feature Symbol, Feature Control Frame, and Hole and Thread Callout** in the 2D environment.

As a QA, I work on both **new and existing functionalities**. For new features, I understand the acceptance criteria from user stories, create test cases and test data, and perform functional and integration testing. I also perform regression testing to make sure existing functionality is not impacted by new changes.

One of the challenges is the complexity of CAD workflows. For example, I test functionality with simple and complex parts, assemblies, and different application modes such as EIP and CIP.

For automation, I mainly use **Playwright and TypeScript** to automate end-to-end workflows, including complex and sometimes slow CAD operations.
